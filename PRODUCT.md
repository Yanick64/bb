# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

BB is currently a personal workbench for a developer at 2DIGITS. Team accounts, invitations and assignment are outside the current product scope.

## Product purpose

Turn client tickets and error reports into investigated, approved, implemented and reviewed work. The operator must understand the original request and the next decision without searching across tools.

## Operating context

Projects open as kanban boards, with a list option. Cross-project action queues use task lists. Teamleader Orbit clients and Sentry projects route incoming work; Google Mail conversations are selected manually. Amp executes remote investigations and implementation with project-specific AI context and communication preferences.

## Capabilities and constraints

- Preserve client content, conversations, original source links and existing local work.
- Keep the workflow Inbox, Triage, Planning, In progress, Review, Delivery, Done.
- Automatically continue after blocking answers, but require human approval for changed plans and completed work.
- Draft delivery messages from observed results. Do not claim deployment or sent messages without evidence.
- Keep time registration out of the interface. Preserve historical records in storage.
- Clearly distinguish a failed launch from a remote execution whose observer was lost.

## Brand commitments

The interface is Dutch. Client drafts follow the client's language. 2DIGITS remains the brand reference, but readable operational typography takes priority over copying its marketing fonts.

## Product principles

- Show the client's original request before internal controls.
- Give each task one obvious next action.
- Explain what failed and provide the relevant recovery action.
- Keep personal use simple. Do not advertise future account features.

## Evidence on hand

The local database contains real projects and source signals. Customer content and credentials stay outside Git. Committed screenshots and test fixtures use synthetic examples.
